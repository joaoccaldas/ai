ond.ai | Spatial Studio
A curated, interactive selection prepared for conversation with Greatlinq AB.

OPENING
Open index.html through a static web host. No installation, account or backend is needed.
Choose an automatic guided tour (90 seconds, 3 minutes or 7 minutes), or explore independently.

INTERACTIVE CONTENT
Visit Worlds to follow links to public source experiences.
Visit Objects to rotate selected public 3D model files when browser 3D/CDN support permits.
Visit Transform to compare authored day and night environments.
Visit Possibilities for three illustrative future collaboration ideas.
