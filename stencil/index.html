See it before you ink

  1. Pick a canvas. Upload a photo of where the tattoo will go, or use a sample skin.
  2. Add a design. Choose from the flash library, the AI pack, or upload your own art.
  3. Place it. Drag to move, pull a corner to resize, use the top handle to rotate.
  4. Blend it. In Adjust, dial in opacity, softness and blend so the ink sits in the skin.
  5. Export. Download a PNG to keep or send to your tattoo artist.
