Interpretation ladder

Every rung requires new evidence

The ladder is not a claim that all evidence moves upward. It is a guardrail against category inflation.

1 · AnthropogenicA human/hominin action produced or positioned the material.
2 · PatternedThe action is structured, repeated or deliberate rather than incidental.
3 · Non-utilitarian?Known pragmatic explanations become less sufficient. The question mark matters.
4 · Symbolic?Material may refer beyond immediate function. Requires interpretive argument.
5 · Ritual?Repeated culturally structured action with social meaning. Symbolic ≠ automatically ritual.
6 · Religious?Cosmology, sacred agents, afterlife or comparable religious content needs independent evidence.
Intentional burial can strongly support mortuary behavior while leaving “afterlife belief” completely underdetermined.
