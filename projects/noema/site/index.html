What NOEMA does

From “what is this?” to “why might these be connected?”

NOEMA combines encyclopedia-style identity with cross-cultural decomposition and evidence-aware analysis. The scientific machinery stays underneath until you ask for it.

01 · IDENTIFY

Who or what?

Names, aliases, places, historical scope, iconography and source-backed reference identity. Think: the clarity of a good encyclopedia card.

02 · DECOMPOSE

What is it made of?

Agency, afterlife, cosmology, ritual grammar, altered-state induction, sacredness, specialists, social function and more.

03 · COMPARE

Where else does it appear?

Compare components across cultures, time and geography, with missingness and source scope kept visible.

04 · TEST

Why might it recur?

Common inheritance, contact, diffusion, convergence, ecology, coding artifacts and chance remain competing explanations until tested.

Why another religion database?

NOEMA is not trying to be only an encyclopedia.

A conventional reference site

  • Tells you what a religion, god or ritual is.
  • Groups material by tradition or topic.
  • Provides texts, images, dates and references.
  • Usually stops before cross-cultural mechanism testing.

NOEMA adds a second layer

  • Breaks traditions into reusable first-principle components.
  • Lets the same component be viewed as entity, map, timeline or graph.
  • Separates resemblance from historical descent or diffusion.
  • Turns recurring patterns into testable hypotheses with rivals and evidence gaps.
