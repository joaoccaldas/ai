Relationship workspace

Connection is not origin.

Reference graph shows explicit catalog associations between named entities. Pattern candidates shows generated DRH feature pairs that survived the current descriptive enrichment screen. Neither mode is a causal-history graph.

A candidate mechanism such as vertical inheritance, horizontal diffusion, migration, trade/contact, syncretism, convergence or local innovation requires a separate evidence-gated relationship record with direction, time plausibility, geography, source support, counterevidence and rivals.
Two edge languages
Reference association

Catalog navigation only. It means NOEMA has explicitly linked the entities in its reference layer.

Pattern candidate

A generated DRH co-occurrence candidate with n, φ, lift, q-value and missingness exclusions.

Selection

Persists the neighborhood or candidate and opens the source-aware inspector.

Blank space

May mean not encoded, not comparable or filtered out. It never automatically means absence.
