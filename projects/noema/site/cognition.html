Historical-role firewall

Witches, shamans, mystics, prophets and mediums are not diagnoses.

NOEMA may study whether documented experiences or social roles resemble mechanisms known today, but accusation records, ritual roles and religious narratives remain historical-cultural evidence. They cannot be converted into autism, ADHD, epilepsy, psychosis or dissociative diagnoses by resemblance.

Biomedical research adds candidate mechanisms. It does not erase culture, meaning, agency or historical uncertainty.
