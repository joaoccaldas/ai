Media + visual provenance

Images are evidence-adjacent, not decoration

NOEMA now has a database/media schema that requires source page, media URL, creator, license, rights status, caption, alt text and entity linkage before an image may be displayed as an approved research asset.

Generated illustrations may be used for explanation, but they must never masquerade as archaeological, historical or ethnographic evidence.
If a blind spot disappears from this page, it should be because a gate was implemented and tested, not because the warning was hidden.
