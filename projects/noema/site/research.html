Discovery mode is intentionally simpler. This section keeps the full scientific and operational trail accessible without forcing every visitor to read it first.

Core rule

Model output is not evidence. Unknown is not absence. Similarity is not ancestry. Biomedical resemblance is not historical diagnosis. Every stronger inference retains source and uncertainty.
