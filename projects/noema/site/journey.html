Odin and Norse magic

What is written about Odin — and is seiðr there?

Odin is in the catalog as a deity, also called Óðinn, Woden, and Wotan. Seiðr is not written on these records. That is a gap, not a verdict that the two are unrelated.

What we have so farEncyclopedia pages for Odin and Thor. The catalog holds 26 names.
What we don't know yet

No dated event. No date on those pages. Pre-Christian Norse religion has no source of its own. What we have not recorded is not the same as false.
