Odin and Norse magic

What is written about Odin — and is seiðr there?

Odin is in the catalog as a deity, also called Óðinn, Woden, and Wotan. Seiðr is not written on these records. That is a gap, not a verdict that the two are unrelated.

What we have so far 2 encyclopedia pages, on 3 records, in a catalog of 26.

What we don't know yet No dated event. No date on those pages. Pre-Christian Norse religion has no source of its own. What we have not recorded is not the same as false.
