## Executive result

This cycle found four graph neighborhoods with material pressure from newly published or newly surfaced evidence:

1. **Supernatural norm enforcement / moralizing-agent models** — scope should be widened beyond high gods and punishment. A new 114-society study reports moralistic supernatural explanations across social scales and across agent classes, with social complexity more strongly associated with supernatural **reward** than punishment.
2. **Cerny mortuary practice ↔ ancestry** — biological admixture is materially downweighted as an explanation for distinctive hunting-associated funerary practices. New genome-wide data show no unusually elevated Mesolithic hunter-gatherer ancestry in the sampled Cerny individuals.
3. **Co-burial ↔ close biological kinship** — materially downweighted. Ancient genomic analysis of early-Christian Swedish collective burials finds children and adults buried together were rarely close biological relatives.
4. **Supernatural-concept transmission** — minimally-counterintuitive/ontological-violation accounts should no longer be treated as the only memory mechanism. New experimental work supports conceptual coherence as a competing explanation for memorability.

Two further areas receive **guardrail/ontology pressure but no hypothesis status change**: (a) predictive-processing accounts of magic are theoretical functional models, not evidence for the function of any specific historical ritual; and (b) neurotheology and magical-thinking literature supports practice-specific biological/psychological mechanisms while strengthening the prohibition on retrospective diagnosis of historical religious actors, alleged witches, shamans, or ritual specialists.

No verified dating revision or species re-attribution was found for NOEMA's focal deep-time chronology this cycle.

Sørensen 2026 proposes that magical ritual applies high-level explanatory priors under causal uncertainty and can coordinate responses across social levels. This is useful theory for hypothesis generation, but it is not a direct test across specific historical cases.

**Action:** separate `BELIEF_CONTENT`, `RITUAL_ACTION`, `UNCERTAINTY/CONTINGENCY_CONTEXT`, `PROPOSED_SOCIAL_FUNCTION`, and `EMPIRICAL_OUTCOME`. Do not promote `MAGIC -> SOCIAL_COHESION` or `MAGIC -> ANXIETY_REDUCTION` as generic facts from this theoretical paper.

`NEURAL_CORRELATE != HISTORICAL_DIAGNOSIS`

`RESEMBLANCE_TO_MODERN_DIAGNOSTIC_FEATURE != RETROSPECTIVE_IDENTITY`

Such hypotheses require independent historical/person-level evidence and category-validity analysis.

## Additional contradiction controls

### Similarity does not uniquely imply diffusion

The northwest-Arabian arrowhead study is useful as a positive control for the graph: Levantine affinities coexist with local innovation. Therefore technological resemblance should not automatically generate migration/descent/diffusion edges. Chronology, contact plausibility, distribution, production sequence and local antecedents must be tested.

### Presence does not establish authorship

Cave-wall ancient DNA establishes that biological traces can survive on wall surfaces. It does not yet identify the artist or bind DNA deposition temporally to pigment application. Future population-attribution edges should require a staged evidence ladder: `HUMAN_PRESENCE -> WALL_CONTACT -> PIGMENT_ASSOCIATION -> TEMPORAL_ASSOCIATION -> MAKER_ATTRIBUTION`.

### Material composition does not establish symbolic motive

Ancient-Egyptian paleoproteomics identifies animal- and plant-derived paint/adhesive materials. Any link from material ingredient to deity, ritual intention, symbolic meaning or workshop theology requires independent contextual/textual/iconographic support.
