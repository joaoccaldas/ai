## 1. Root Cause of Earlier Visual Clutter & Lack of Focus

1. **Boxed 3D Canvas (~35% of Viewport)**: The 3D course was trapped inside a 3-column scrolling grid below a 200px editorial banner and above a 3-column marketing footer.
2. **14-Control Left Sidebar Kitchen Sink**: File import, world presets, bike avatars, photo upload, privacy redaction pins, and tutorial launchers were all stacked with identical visual weight.
3. **5 Competing Telemetry Readouts**: Hero subtitle, tutorial banner, canvas HUD, right-hand Moment cards, and 4-card stage stats all competed for attention simultaneously.
4. **Developer Provenance on Main Stage**: Internal repository paths and contract schemas were rendered on the main page rather than inside an on-demand drawer.

1. **Layer 0 — Full-Bleed 3D Course Sculpture (`100vw × 100vh`, `overflow: hidden`)**:

3. **Layer 2 — On-Demand `Lab & Garage` Slide-Over Drawer (`#labDrawer`)**:
