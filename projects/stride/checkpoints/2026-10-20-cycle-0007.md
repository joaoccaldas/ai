## 1. The Question STRIDE Answers & Why Earlier Iterations Fell Short

**The Core Question**:
> *"Where were the decisive moments of my effort, what did it look and feel like inside that terrain, and why did I gain or lose time there?"*

**Why Earlier Iterations Fell Short**:
1. **Descriptive instead of Causal**: Moment cards only reported raw numbers (`Fastest 1 km: 3:51`) without explaining *why* that kilometer was fastest or slowest (grade %, speed delta vs. average, heart-rate response, or Ghost split swing).
2. **Static Camera on Moment Selection**: Clicking a Moment jumped the playhead dot to a single point without flying the camera into the 1 km corridor or illuminating the segment on the 3D sculpture and 2D profile.
3. **Flat 2D-Looking Route & Dot Avatar**: The route lacked vertical elevation curtains, course portals (`START`, `SUMMIT`, `FINISH`), and an articulated 3D cyclist/bike avatar (`Speedmax CFR AXS 2027`).
4. **Developer-Centric JSON Dump**: `Worlds & Reuse` showed raw `<pre>` JSON payloads first rather than interactive World and KONA.m Bicycle Garage cards.

## 2. What Was Built & Verified in Cycle 0007

1. **Causal Moment Diagnosis Engine (`diagnoseMoments()` in `core.mjs`)**:
   - Synthesizes a 1-sentence **Ride Thesis** (`#rideThesisText`) at the top of the studio (`"Covered 11.1 km at 9.4 km/h avg; peaked in Km 1 (15.6 km/h) and crested the steepest ramp in Km 5 (+49 m, +4.9% grade) — finishing 0:16 ahead of Ghost."`).
   - Enriches every Moment (`Fastest 1 km`, `Steepest 1 km Gain`, `Slowest 1 km`, `Largest Ghost Gap Split`) with a causal `why` breakdown (speed delta % vs. ride average, climb/descent grade %, heart-rate response vs. average, and per-kilometer Ghost split swings).
2. **True 3D Course Sculpture, Camera Fly-To & Articulated 3D Avatar (`app.mjs`)**:
   - **Smooth Camera Fly-To (`flyToMoment`)**: Selecting any Moment smoothly interpolates camera focus, zoom (`1.52×`), pitch, and yaw onto that 1 km corridor while illuminating the active segment (`state.highlightRange`) on both the 3D terrain and 2D elevation profile.
   - **3D Vertical Elevation Curtain**: Translucent vertical curtain and kilometer stanchions dropping from the 3D GPS ribbon to the terrain floor, colored by climb/effort intensity.
   - **3D Course Portals**: `START`, `▲ SUMMIT`, and `FINISH` 3D arch gates straddling the course.
   - **Articulated 3D Cyclist / Runner Avatar (`drawAthleteAvatar`)**: Renders two 3D wheels, an aero diamond frame colored by the selected KONA.m bike (`CFR-2027`, `CF-SLX-2027`, `CF-2011`), tucked rider torso + aero helmet, and floating live telemetry pill (`CFR-2027 · 96m · 147bpm`).
   - **Multi-Part 3D Procedural Studio Props (`drawProceduralProp`)**: 4-frond curved 3D Kona palms, 3-tier Nice alpine conifers, and faceted St. George sandstone spires with ground shadows.
   - **Floating Causal Moment HUD (`#causalHud`)**: Real-time overlay inside the 3D viewport explaining *why* the current kilometer or selected Moment unfolded as it did.
3. **Interactive Worlds & KONA.m Bicycle Garage (`renderWorldsAndContracts()`)**:
   - Replaced raw JSON dumps with interactive World cards (`Enter World in 3D →`) and KONA.m Bicycle Atlas cards (`Equip Bike in 3D →`), keeping raw `ActivitySpec v1` & `MomentSpec v1` JSON accessible inside a collapsible `<details>` inspector.
