# A1 Jury Board — Final Composition Contract

## Goal
The board must make the thesis legible in ~10 seconds, the architecture understandable in ~30 seconds, and the evidence inspectable in ~2 minutes.

## Visual hierarchy
1. **Hero — 45–55% of visual attention**
   - human-eye market/civic scene;
   - Sagrada visual primacy;
   - real canonical geometry;
   - no decorative fog or invented spatial content.
2. **Authoritative plan — 12–15%**
   - full plaza;
   - program split;
   - public/service flows;
   - north + scale;
   - Sagrada relationship.
3. **Decisive section — 12–15%**
   - 9 m clear structural span;
   - human scale;
   - passive strategy;
   - market + civic edge;
   - viewpoint relationship.
4. **Gaudí + AI logic — 10–12%**
   - force/material logic;
   - Cerdà-derived geometric method;
   - option generation -> rejection -> human decision;
   - no decorative Gaudí checklist.
5. **Evidence strip — 8–10%**
   - only validated metrics;
   - site/context proof;
   - environmental proof;
   - compact buildability/detail evidence.
6. **Micro-detail — 5–8%**
   - J01 roof/edge/column or Threshold Stall interface.

## One-sentence thesis
The Living Threshold turns the Sagrada Família plaza from a tourist foreground into a daily civic market and meeting ground, using Gaudí's structural logic and evidence-driven AI iteration while preserving the basilica's visual primacy.

## Exclusion rules
- no duplicate diagram;
- no metric without provenance;
- no visual that cannot answer a likely jury question;
- no paragraph that can be replaced by a diagram;
- no second hero competing with the primary image;
- no personal name or identifying metadata.

## Final gate
A1 cannot be approved until SITE_TRUTH_V1, canonical scene, structural/environmental evidence and hero gate are all approved.
