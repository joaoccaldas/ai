# Gaudí Nexus — Winning Pass Plan

**Date:** 2026-10-07  
**Branch:** `gaudi-winning-pass-20261007`  
**Base:** `gaudi-nexus`  
**Objective:** Convert the Rev18 site-truth candidate into a jury-ready, evidence-backed submission without reopening the architectural concept.

## Decision

Do not spend the next cycle on decorative object count.

The project is now constrained by four high-leverage gaps:

1. final site-truth promotion;
2. fast, inspectable photoreal hero iteration;
3. authoritative plan + decisive section;
4. compact structural/environmental proof on the A1.

The work should proceed in that order because each later artifact depends on the truth of the previous layer.

---

## Track A — SITE_TRUTH_V1 closure

### Current evidence
- Rev18 site-truth candidate built successfully.
- Authoritative municipal 3D collection integrated.
- F3 hero camera installed.
- Proxy context retained for audit and hidden from competition diagnostics.
- Candidate blend and report hashes recorded.

### Remaining gates
1. obtain the official 2026 Barcelona municipal topographic GeoPackage through an authorized path;
2. checksum exact bytes;
3. inventory CRS/layers;
4. crop the verified 300 m competition context;
5. reconcile levels, curbs, tree points and urban furniture against Rev18;
6. recompute Passion-façade sightline;
7. recompute shadows;
8. explicitly promote the resulting binary lineage to `SITE_TRUTH_V1`.

### Why first
Plan, section, landscaping, accessibility, hero framing and environmental evidence all inherit site geometry. A beautiful board built before this gate can become expensive rework.

### Fail-closed rule
No generic terrain, hand-modeled curbs or guessed street furniture may be substituted for unavailable official data.

---

## Track B — F3 fast photoreal review

### Goal
Get from camera/material change to inspectable preview quickly enough to iterate.

### Implementation
- rebuild Rev18 from checksum-pinned Rev15 + municipal inputs;
- validate semantic identity, not byte-for-byte Blender serialization;
- render F3 at 640×360 and 960×540 in Eevee;
- record render time/resource use;
- preserve geometry, camera, transforms and materials;
- hide only the already-rejected proxy context;
- hash every preview and receipt.

### Exit gate
A reviewer can inspect actual pixels and score:
- Sagrada primacy;
- Living Threshold identity;
- depth and human scale;
- material realism;
- contact/intersection errors;
- foreground choreography;
- truth consistency.

Only after this loop is fast and stable should we move to expensive final hero rendering.

---

## Track C — A1 alpha now

### Goal
Use the board as a missing-argument detector.

### Alpha hierarchy
1. hero: 45–55%;
2. authoritative plan: 12–15%;
3. decisive section: 12–15%;
4. Gaudí + AI system logic: 10–12%;
5. evidence strip: 8–10%;
6. one micro-detail/buildability proof: 5–8%.

### Current alpha policy
- current verified F3 preview may occupy the hero cell;
- unresolved plan/section remain visibly marked PENDING;
- no invented metrics;
- no decorative diagram added merely to fill space;
- readiness percentages are labelled as project-management estimates, never jury scores.

### Why now
If the thesis cannot be read on the board, more model detail is unlikely to fix it.

---

## Track D — plan + section

Begin immediately after SITE_TRUTH geometry is frozen.

### Plan must show
- full plaza;
- building footprint and programme split;
- market/service flows;
- public/service access;
- north and scale;
- relationship to Passion façade;
- retained/new landscape;
- authoritative context boundary.

### Section must prove
- 9 m structural span;
- human scale;
- market/civic relationship;
- roof/ceramic solar logic;
- ventilation path;
- drainage;
- viewpoint relationship;
- one credible support/joint condition.

### Exit gate
Both drawings derive from the same canonical scene as the hero.

---

## Track E — jury-relevant physics only

### Structural
- funicular/form-finding logic;
- support reactions;
- load-path diagram;
- one resolved joint/movement condition;
- assumptions labelled.

### Environmental
- annual solar/daylight;
- ventilation method;
- rainwater basis;
- market acoustics;
- optional embodied-carbon comparison if it materially strengthens the material decision.

### Why limited scope
The competition rewards architectural credibility, not a complete construction package.

- increase a named jury criterion with defensible evidence;
