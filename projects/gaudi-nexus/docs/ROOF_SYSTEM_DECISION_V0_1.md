# Roof System Decision V0.1

## Options tested
A. Pure thin-tile vault  
B. Precast ribbed shell  
C. Hybrid thin-tile shell + mineral/precast edge frame  
D. Timber/steel catenary roof

## Decision
**C is primary. D is the engineering fallback.**

The selection optimizes:
- Gaudí-method fidelity;
- buildability;
- repeatability;
- digital fabrication;
- maintenance;
- visual quality;
- schedule resilience.

## Why not pure historical replication
The Sagrada Família itself combines Catalan vaulting, reinforced concrete, prefabrication and contemporary digital fabrication. The lesson is not to freeze a historical technique, but to combine geometry, material and modern fabrication intelligently.

## Kill criterion
If structural analysis shows the masonry/composite shell cannot meet asymmetric load, vibration, durability or construction constraints without excessive reinforcement/complexity, switch to fallback D while preserving the spatial geometry.
