# Code Screen V0.2 — 2026-10-04

## Purpose
Translate the primary scheme into conservative concept-stage fire/egress/accessibility geometry using the current Spanish CTE as the authority.

This is **not final regulatory certification**.

## Current authoritative references
- CTE DB-SI, current fire-safety document including RD 164/2025 modifications.
- CTE DB-SUA, current accessibility/safety document.
- Current DB-SI comments and current DB-SUA comments are explanatory aids, not substitutes for the regulatory text.

## Occupancy density assumptions used for the screen
- market/public sales: 2.0 m²/person;
- museum / interpretation / exhibition public zones: 2.0 m²/person;
- multipurpose/community hall: 1.0 m²/person;
- seated/cooking public zone conservative proxy: 1.5 m²/person;
- admin/staff zones: 10 m²/person;
- plant/maintenance-only: occupancy treated as nil for this concept screen.

Where the final activity differs, the density must be replaced by the closest actual CTE category.

## Screened floor occupancies
- Hard bar L0: ~160 people.
- Hard bar L1: ~231 people.
- Civic bar L0: ~291 people.
- Civic bar L1: ~300 people.

## Width formulas applied
Current DB-SI SI 3 uses:
- doors/passes: A >= P/200, minimum 0.80 m;
- corridors/ramps: A >= P/200, minimum 1.00 m;
- non-protected descending stairs: A >= P/160.

## Current design reservations
- Hard bar: two 1.50 m clear stairs; 1.50 m main public route.
- Civic bar: two 2.00 m clear stairs are drawn in the current plan, but the conservative 300-person upper-floor screen would require ~1.875 m by the simple non-protected formula, so 2.00 m is the minimum current design intent and 2.40 m is reserved in the code-screen allowance until the protected-stair strategy is resolved.
- Main accessible circulation is targeted at >=1.50 m, above the DB-SUA accessible-itinerary minimum of 1.20 m.
- Complex maneuver points retain Ø1.50 m clear turning space.

## Travel distance
Two end exits in a 45 m bar give a longitudinal worst-case distance of roughly 22.5 m to the nearer end before local cross-room travel.

This is promising against the current DB-SI two-exit maximum of 50 m, but **every final origin of evacuation must be checked after room partitions are frozen.**
