## Market stall test
A first packing attempt returned **0/20 stalls for both concepts**.

That was not a design failure. It exposed a bad assumption in the test itself: stalls had been oriented with 4.0 m depth across the bands.

The test was corrected to:
- stall module: 4.0 m along the market length × 2.4 m depth;
- central aisle proxy: 3.0 m;
- service/support zone reserved at one end of the first component.

### Corrected result
- C+: 20/20 stalls fit.
- A+ V0.3: 20/20 stalls fit.

This is only geometric feasibility. Counter depth, refrigeration, wash-down, fire strategy, accessible stalls, storage and actual trader mix remain unresolved.
