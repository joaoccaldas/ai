# Structural Bay V0.1

## Intent
Translate Gaudí's method through load path and constructive intelligence rather than visual imitation.

## Current structural hypothesis
- 10.0 m transverse clear span;
- 5.0 m longitudinal structural rhythm;
- catenary-informed roof geometry;
- target roof rise ~1.4 m over the 10 m span;
- repetitive edge supports / frames;
- column-free market aisle and stalls.

## Material hypothesis
- primary edge frames: low-carbon mineral / precast concrete or equivalent;
- roof: thin tile/mineral vault or tile used as permanent formwork/composite layer where engineering validates it;
- replaceable ceramic/terracotta solar-control layer;
- durable mineral/reused civic paving.

## Rule
No curve survives merely because it looks Gaudí-like.

A curved/ruled surface must improve at least one of:
- load path;
- span/depth;
- daylight;
- ventilation;
- acoustic behavior;
- drainage;
- solar control;
- human spatial quality.

## Not yet proven
- thrust magnitude;
- lateral stability;
- foundation reactions;
- fire resistance;
- seismic/wind response;
- shell thickness;
- material embodied carbon;
- connection detailing.

Those remain structural-engineering tasks.
