## What changed in this gate
The previous plan-only decision was subjected to:
- step-free access / two-core egress feasibility;
- market service-path tests;
- conservative solar-shadow proxies;
- human-scale section sketches;
- explicit structural load-path hypotheses.

## Critical finding 1 — A had another serious defect
A V0.2 had excellent sampled sightline performance but poor operations and winter solar behavior:
- service distance ≈ 85.6 m;
- winter 09:00 shadow proxy ≈ 78.5%.

The concept was not defended.

A V0.3 relocates the two bars:
- service distance ≈ 10.0 m;
- winter 09:00 shadow ≈ 8.9%;
- summer noon shadow ≈ 8.9%;
- sampled sightline obstruction remains 0°;
- protected-view overlap remains 0 m².

The civic thesis survived. The geometry changed.

## Critical finding 2 — C servicing is better than first measured
The first C service diagram incorrectly distributed toward the whole concept centroid and crossed ~17.6 m of the civic-center zone.

After designating one component as the explicit hard-service band:
- service entry → band ≈ 36.6 m;
- civic-zone route overlap drops to ≈ 3.6 m.

This still fails our desired 0 m service/civic overlap target and must improve again.

## Accessibility / egress screen
We are not claiming building-code compliance yet.

Current concept targets:
- 1.5 m main accessible circulation;
- step-free ground floor;
- public upper levels lift-served;
- two independent vertical escape cores;
- 700-person stress occupancy for robustness testing only.

Current Spanish CTE DB-SUA and DB-SI remain the authority for detailed verification.
