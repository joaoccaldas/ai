# Quality bar

## Geometry
- Model in real units.
- Explicit tolerance classes: context, architecture, joinery/detail.
- No non-manifold final export geometry unless intentionally documented.
- Plan/section/render all derive from the same authoritative model.

## Visuals
- Cameras are versioned with focal length, sensor assumptions, position, target and crop.
- Final renders use managed color, physically plausible materials and repeatable light rigs.
- Every hero image must answer a design question, not merely look attractive.
- Every analytical diagram must show cause → effect.

## Board test
At three viewing distances:
1. 3–4 m: thesis and hero must be legible.
2. 1–1.5 m: architecture and major diagrams must read.
3. 30–50 cm: technical evidence, captions and process must reward inspection.

## Anti-AI-slop checks
- no impossible structure hidden by atmosphere;
- no inconsistent openings between views;
- no people at contradictory scale;
- no unexplained decorative biomorphism;
- no fake environmental arrows unsupported by analysis;
- no render whose geometry differs from plan/section.
