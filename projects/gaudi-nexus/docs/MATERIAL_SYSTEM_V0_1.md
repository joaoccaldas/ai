# Material System V0.1

## Rule
No material enters the final project because it merely "looks Gaudí."

Every material must first solve at least one real problem:
- structure;
- solar control;
- thermal mass;
- hygiene;
- repair;
- durability;
- touch;
- acoustic quality;
- or maintenance.

## Candidate system
- **Primary frame:** low-carbon mineral / hybrid frame. Short spans, durability and thermal mass; embodied carbon still to benchmark.
- **Secondary roof:** engineered timber plus thin mineral/ceramic layer. Warm soffit, repairable, lightweight; fire/moisture detailing required.
- **Shading skin:** replaceable fired-ceramic modules. Solar control and tactile identity; avoid trencadís imitation.
- **Market wet zone:** dense mineral / terrazzo-type floor with drainage logic. Hygiene, washdown and impact resistance.
- **Stall kit:** stainless/galvanized frame plus timber touch surfaces. Food hygiene plus modular replacement.
- **Landscape:** retain/new shade trees and permeable ground. Microclimate is functional infrastructure.

## Gaudí translation
Material hierarchy follows forces and use. Ornament is never the justification.
