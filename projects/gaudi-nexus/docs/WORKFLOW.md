# End-to-end workflow

## Workstreams
A. **Truth** — brief, site, precedent, jury-independent research.
B. **Architecture** — program, circulation, structure, envelope, public realm.
C. **Physics** — solar, wind, acoustics, structural logic, crowd flow.
D. **Computation** — parametric model, variant engine, optimization, provenance.
E. **Representation** — cameras, Cycles, diagrams, board, print QA.

## Stage sequence
1. Brief lock.
2. Site truth.
3. Five divergent concepts.
4. Low-cost evaluation and fatal-flaw elimination.
5. Two concept finalists.
6. Structural/environmental proof.
7. One selected scheme.
8. Unified BIM-like Blender authority model.
9. Pixel-level rendering + diagram production.
10. A1 composition and three-distance critique.
11. Red-team review, anonymity/IP/file checks.
12. Submit with ≥24 h buffer.

## Anti-Goodhart rule
The 100-point rubric is a review instrument. It may reject weak designs, but it does not generate the design. Any optimization objective that can be gamed by geometry must be paired with qualitative human review.
