# Competition visual system

## Board visual grammar
- One dominant hero, never a collage of equal images.
- Technical drawings are monochrome/near-monochrome with one analytical accent system.
- Diagrams show an input, transformation, and consequence.
- AI process evidence is compact and auditable, not prompt screenshots as decoration.

## Candidate hero cameras
1. **Resident eye / market morning** — 28–35 mm equivalent, 1.55 m eye, Sagrada visible but not theatrically centered. Proves daily life + monument coexistence.
2. **Section-perspective** — cut through market/community/viewpoint with light and air arrows derived from analysis. Proves architecture.
3. **Elevated urban oblique** — 45–60 mm equivalent, enough height to read plaza reconfiguration without drone-game aesthetics. Proves city logic.
4. **Night community scene** — only if operational and lighting strategy are strong enough to justify scarce board area.

## Material target
Barcelona-specific mineral/ceramic/timber/metal palette should be justified by durability, heat, touch, maintenance and local construction culture, not Gaudí mimicry.
