# Critical Path — Next 10 Steps

## 1. Move to a private dedicated repository
**Why now:** the current staging branch is public. Final concepts, authorship clues, research notes and unpublished competition work should not remain public.

**Done when:**
- dedicated private repository exists;
- branch history and provenance are preserved;
- public staging branch contains no final/private competition assets.

**Dependency:** none.
**Kill condition:** do not upload final anonymous board/assets to the public staging repo.

## 2. Replace secondary context with authoritative municipal site data
**Why:** every view, shadow, level, tree and public-realm decision depends on this.

**Required:**
- Barcelona 1:1000 topographic GPKG;
- official 3D Sagrada Família neighborhood model;
- competition-issued drawings/assets;
- exact plaza boundary, curb levels, trees, street furniture and entrances.

**Done when:** `SITE_TRUTH_V1.0` is generated from authoritative sources and secondary OSM/CARTO layers become cross-checks only.

## 3. Establish one authoritative Blender model
**Why:** plan, section, render, 3D and diagram coherence is currently distributed across scripts/SCAD/GLB.

**Done when:**
- Blender scene is generated reproducibly from versioned inputs;
- A+ bars, cores, stalls, bay system, trees/context and camera registry live in one spatial authority;
- every plan/section/render derives from it.

## 4. Validate the roof structurally
**Why:** the shell is the strongest Gaudí-method claim and therefore the most dangerous place to bluff.

**Required:**
- funicular/form-finding model;
- support/thrust sensitivity;
- shell/rib comparison;
- deflection/buckling/material assumptions;
- fallback system decision.

**Done when:** the 9 m span is either validated for continued development or replaced without changing the civic concept.

## 5. Replace environmental proxies with validated simulation
**Why:** passive design is a scored competition category and currently only partially proven.

**Required:**
- annual solar/shade;
- daylight/UDI or daylight-factor evidence;
- wind/comfort study;
- rain sizing;
- acoustic zoning/reverberation;
- thermal/passive strategy.

**Done when:** no final board arrow claims performance unsupported by a result.

## 6. Close code, accessibility and operations
**Why:** a beautiful public market that cannot evacuate, serve traders or operate independently is not credible architecture.

**Required:**
- every origin-to-exit path;
- protected/non-protected stair decision;
- lift and accessible route;
- WCs;
- kitchen/workshop;
- cold/dry storage;
- waste;
- loading;
- independent evening mode.

**Done when:** one complete plan/section package passes an internal CTE checklist with assumptions documented.

## 7. Resolve one 5 m bay to near-construction credibility
**Why:** this is the building's DNA and the fastest route to realism.

**Required joints:**
- pier→edge beam→shell;
- shell→waterproofing→gutter;
- fin bracket;
- lighting/MEP access;
- acoustic panel;
- stall quick connector;
- drain;
- paving expansion;
- maintenance access.

**Done when:** no visible component in the hero camera lacks a plausible attachment, thickness or maintenance path.

## 8. Produce the first competition-grade human scene
**Why:** the largest remaining weakness is emotion and visual quality.

**Target:**
- 28–35 mm;
- eye height ~1.55 m;
- ordinary Barcelona morning;
- resident/trader/visitor coexistence;
- material tactility;
- Sagrada relationship visible but not wallpaper;
- Cycles-quality light/material response.

**Kill condition:** if the scene still reads as generic pavilion architecture, revise the architecture before polishing the render.

## 9. Build A1 V1 early and design backward from it
**Why:** the jury receives one board, not the repo.

**Proposed hierarchy:**
- hero 35–40%;
- section ~20%;
- plan ~15%;
- three compact diagrams;
- one construction detail;
- compact AI/evidence strip.

**Done when:** thesis reads at 3–4 m, architecture at 1 m, technical proof close-up.

## 10. Red-team, simplify, freeze and submit early
**Why:** late additions are more dangerous than missing cleverness.

**Required:**
- independent architectural critique;
- structural/environmental contradiction audit;
- anonymity/IP/provenance scrub;
- board file-size/export test;
- final official deadline/timezone check;
- submission ≥24 h early.

**Final rule:** after freeze, only defects and contradictions may change.
