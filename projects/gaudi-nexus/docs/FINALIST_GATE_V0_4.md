# Finalist Gate V0.4 — 2026-10-04

## Program fit
Both finalists can contain the required program in principle.

### A+ Living Threshold V0.3
Ground floor now contains concept blocks for:
- cold/dry/waste/staff;
- 20 market stalls + aisle;
- public WC / prep;
- flexible seating / queue;
- community kitchen;
- neighborhood room / seating.

Upper level retains:
- interpretation + viewpoint;
- community / cooking;
- admin;
- flexible / seasonal use.

Key quality:
the ~10 m-deep bars leave materially more planning slack and can support two-sided public frontage.

### C+ Cerdà Mutation
Ground floor now contains:
- cold/dry/waste/staff;
- 20 stalls + aisle split across two narrow bands;
- WC/access core;
- queue/storage/core zones.

Upper levels remain viable in area but the ~5.6 m band width remains the core weakness:
excellent cross-ventilation and daylight depth, but low tolerance for real rooms, acoustic separation and service complexity.

## Evening operation
Both can keep civic uses open after the food market closes.

A+ is cleaner:
- ~443 m² ground-floor civic edge can remain independently open;
- the hard market/service edge can close without reopening back-of-house;
- the central plaza remains public.

C+ can do the same in principle, but its stronger vertical program dependency makes zoning and independent operation less natural.

## Daylight-depth proxy
No lux claims are made.

- A+ representative clear width: ~10.0 m.
  - deepest plan point: ~5.0 m from exterior.
- C+ representative clear width: ~5.6 m.
  - deepest plan point: ~2.8 m from exterior.

Interpretation:
C is exceptionally shallow.
A remains shallow enough for a robust two-sided daylight strategy while providing far better program flexibility.

## Material system V0.1
The project rejects a generic “Gaudí aesthetic”.

Current hierarchy:
1. low-carbon mineral / concrete primary structure;
2. selective Catalan brick/tile vault modules where span/acoustics justify them;
3. ceramic/terracotta shading elements sized by solar need;
4. durable/reused mineral ground plane;
5. demountable stainless + timber market kit.

The Sagrada Família is relevant methodologically because it combines stone, reinforced concrete, Catalan vaulting and digitally enabled fabrication. The lesson is advanced construction + material hierarchy, not surface imitation.

## Decision
**Select Living Threshold as the primary scheme.**

Confidence: **medium**.

Do not discard C and E:
- C becomes the **geometric method donor**.
- E remains the **market/service metabolism donor**.

The synthesis hierarchy is explicit:
- **A = architecture**
- **C = method**
- **E = subsystem**

This avoids concept soup.

## Primary scheme definition
The architecture remains:
- two low inhabited edges;
- one hard market/service bar;
- one civic/community/interpretation bar;
- an unprogrammed civic middle.

C contributes one rule:
> start from the Eixample/Cerdà order and deform geometry only when public flow, protected views, structural load path, climate/daylight or servicing justify it.

E contributes:
- concentrated wet/service cores;
- delivery/storage/waste/exhaust logic;
- modular market infrastructure.

## Why A now wins
1. lower and quieter urban section;
2. stronger neighborhood/public-space thesis;
3. better current market flexibility;
4. cleaner service edge;
5. cleaner evening operation;
6. good daylight depth without C’s corridor risk;
7. sampled view protection preserved;
8. computational logic can be imported without importing C’s entire form.

## Remaining kill conditions
The primary scheme is not frozen yet.

It dies or is radically revised if:
- support spaces consume the market flexibility;
- independent egress cannot be resolved cleanly;
- real municipal heights/topography overturn the environmental assumptions;
- acoustic separation between tourist and neighborhood uses fails;
- the first material/light spatial scene feels generic;
- the two bars read as disconnected pavilions rather than one civic sequence.

## Regulatory note
Current design checks are concept screens only. Final compliance must be verified against the current CTE, especially DB-SUA for safe, non-discriminatory access and DB-SI for fire safety/egress.
