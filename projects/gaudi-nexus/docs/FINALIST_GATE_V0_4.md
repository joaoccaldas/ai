# Finalist Gate V0.4 — 2026-10-04

## Current finalists
1. **A+ Living Threshold V0.3**
2. **C+ Cerdà Mutation**

## New evidence

### A+ multi-objective iteration
The earlier A+ V0.2 cleared sampled view rays but performed badly on service distance and low-winter-sun shadow proxy.

V0.3 uses the same civic thesis but relocates the two bars.

Measured concept proxies:
- footprint: ~888 m²;
- two levels: ~1,775 m² total GFA;
- sampled protected-view overlap: 0 m²;
- sampled view-ray obstruction: 0°;
- service edge to hard-service bar: ~10 m;
- winter 09:00 conservative shadow envelope: ~8.9% of plaza;
- summer noon conservative shadow envelope: ~8.9% of plaza.

### C+
C remains the stronger abstract Gaudí × Cerdà systems thesis, but its narrow ~5.6 m bands create a serious flexibility challenge.

## Market capacity
After correcting the first stall-packing test, both finalists geometrically fit 20 concept modules of 4.0 × 2.4 m with a 3.0 m aisle proxy and a service reserve.

The important difference is residual flexibility:
- C+: ~11% of ground footprint remains after the simplified stall/aisle/service packing;
- A+: ~42% remains.

This does not prove market operation, but it is a strong signal that A+ can absorb queueing, seating, stall diversity and seasonal change more easily.

## Decision
A+ is the current provisional leader. C+ remains alive because its core architectural idea is more distinctive and can still outperform A+ if section, structure and spatial experience become exceptional.

## Next kill gate
Both concepts must now prove:
- internally consistent floor-by-floor program;
- toilets / storage / waste / service / staff spaces;
- accessibility + independent egress strategy;
- 24-hour operating modes;
- daylight depth;
- buildable structural bay;
- candidate material logic;
- one deterministic 3D human-scale scene.
