# Asset Library V1 — 27 Deterministic 3D Components

The Living Threshold world now has a reusable component library rather than ad-hoc scene clutter.

## Why
The project was visually reading as a prototype because everything outside the primary structure was generic massing.

A believable civic market needs a second layer of architecture:
- market kit;
- service equipment;
- public furniture;
- accessibility elements;
- drainage;
- lighting;
- signage;
- landscape;
- maintenance equipment.

These elements now exist as dimensioned GLB assets.

## Rule
Every component must:
- have real dimensions;
- have a defined operational or environmental job;
- be removable/repairable when appropriate;
- use a PBR material family compatible with the primary model;
- not exist merely as decoration.

## Current status
Geometry is design-development quality.
Final textures, wear, manufacturing detail and Blender/Cycles material calibration are still pending.
