# World V0.8 / V0.9 Visual Gate

## V0.8
Generated a full downloadable GLB world with:
- real plaza relationship;
- current A+ architecture;
- 5 m structural rhythm;
- catenary roof;
- fins;
- 20 stalls;
- produce;
- trees;
- benches;
- people;
- context;
- provisional Sagrada proxy.

Geometry count: **380**.

## V0.9
Rendered a new human-eye hero camera and explicitly failed it as a winning image.

Why it fails:
- municipal context/Sagrada remain proxies;
- material response is too schematic;
- facade articulation is under-resolved;
- human entourage is still placeholder-grade;
- spatial experience is calmer and clearer, but not emotionally unforgettable;
- render engine is not Cycles/production archviz quality.

## Decision
**Do not call the product winning yet.**

This is an important gate: the world is now complex enough to expose the actual remaining problem.
The bottleneck is not more objects. It is authoritative context + physically convincing materials/light + stronger spatial composition.
