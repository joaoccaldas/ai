## Make the architecture read

Review silhouette, section, human scale and spatial depth before materials.
Keep one main subject per view. A massing source cannot become a detailed
Sagrada Família model through shader invention. Retain the documented municipal
geometry and disclose its level of detail. Do not hide unresolved geometry behind
fog, a photograph or generated pixels.

Review materials at their actual distance: grain/roughness in metres, contact,
edge thickness and useful construction joints. A close illustrative figure can
dominate the quality judgment of a whole market; use quieter, more distant
entourage when that source cannot sustain a close view. Avoid multiplying props
that do not explain daily use or the construction system.

## Repeat the current pipeline

1. Pin the municipal source, prepared scene and adjacent receipts. Check all
   required external images/libraries; absent `_tex/provenance.json` means no
   claim that CC0 scanned textures were loaded. The present coordination palette
   uses explicit procedural materials; portable exports are PBR approximations.
2. Prepare derived geometry without rendering on this Mac. Preserve all original
   geometry/transforms and files; check the same source signatures, required
   assets, real floor contacts, programme areas and sampled camera clearances.
3. Use the existing approved Linux Actions renderer, one process at a time,
   within its bounded budget. It verifies the prepared receipt and rejects
   invented cathedral enrichment. No paid provider or local-render fallback is
   implied. The current renderer uses Cycles CPU, adaptive sampling, denoising,
   AgX and 16-bit PNG, with declared Barcelona date/time assumptions.
4. Compare unchanged cameras, dimensions, profile and sample caps against the
   source-bound baseline. Keep raw images; any `finish.py` treatment is a separate
   disclosed derivative. Pixel range catches collapsed outputs, not artistic
   quality. Inspect all saved images, including detail and wider context.
5. Record the prediction before review, then the result and contrary evidence in
   `RESEARCH.json`. A failed look is retained and corrected in its owning source.
   Geometry, glTF validation, source identity, image quality and target-device
   behavior are separate gates. None substitutes for independent acceptance.

## Competition frame

Keep the A1 hierarchy in `visuals/A1_STORYBOARD.md`: one dominant human-eye
image, a full-plaza plan, a decisive section and a compact evidence/AI process
strip. Diagrams derive from the same geometry and carry scale and provenance.
New assets must explain a programme, spatial or environmental need. Current
organizer scope permits a flat-site conceptual assumption; structural/code,
acoustic and hydraulic certification must not be inferred from a plausible image.
Keep the source limit conflict and stricter 10 MB working cap explicit. No
participant identifier, owner approval, submission or competition result is
invented.
