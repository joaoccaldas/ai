# Maintenance / Replacement Strategy V0.1

## Principle
The project distinguishes **long-life architecture** from **short-life operational equipment**.

### Long-life
- foundations;
- piers;
- edge beams;
- compression shell;
- civic ground.

### Medium / short-life
- market stall;
- lights;
- acoustic panels;
- ceramic fins;
- gutters/downpipes;
- MEP rail components;
- identity/display panels.

## Bay-level sequence
1. **Isolate** one 5 m bay.
2. **Unplug** stall wet / power / data quick connectors.
3. **Remove** the market kit without altering primary structure.
4. **Inspect** drain, gutter, lighting, acoustic strips, fins and MEP rail.
5. **Replace only the failed layer.**
6. **Recommission** the bay.

Adjacent bays remain operational.

## Why it matters
The market can evolve without forcing the public architecture to become obsolete.

This supports the central thesis:
**the plaza and structure are permanent civic infrastructure; commercial fit-out is adaptable.**
