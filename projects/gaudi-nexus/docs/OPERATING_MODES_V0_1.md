# Operating Modes V0.1

The project is not allowed to exist only in a competition render.

## Daily states

### 06:00–09:00 — trader setup
- service entrance active;
- cold/dry storage to stalls;
- waste return separated;
- civic middle remains passable.

### 09:00–14:00 — market peak
- full stall operation;
- maximum queue and seating load;
- shade and accessibility most critical.

### 14:00–18:00 — mixed civic
- reduced market load;
- cooking/community rooms active;
- deliberate resident/visitor overlap.

### 18:00–22:00 — community evening
- wet-market edge partially shutters;
- community and interpretation stay open independently;
- deliveries stop.

### 22:00–06:00 — closed / maintenance
- public rooms secure;
- cleaning/waste windows possible;
- plaza remains legible and passable.

## Architectural consequence
The hard-service market edge must be operationally independent from the civic/interpretation edge. Closing the market must not turn the entire intervention into a dead object.
