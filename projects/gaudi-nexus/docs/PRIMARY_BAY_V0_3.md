# Primary Bay V0.3 — Physical Detail Gate

## What changed
Primary Bay V0.3 resolves the first two explicit construction interfaces rather than adding more conceptual form.

### J01 — roof / edge / column
Now separated into maintainable layers:
- primary pier;
- edge beam / springing block;
- replaceable bearing / movement layer;
- catenary-informed compression shell;
- continuous waterproofing / flashing;
- removable gutter;
- accessible downpipe / cleanout;
- bolted ceramic-fin bracket;
- replaceable solar-control fin;
- accessible MEP + lighting rail;
- acoustic insert.

### J02 — stall / floor / service
The market kit is explicitly independent from the building:
- demountable stainless chassis;
- wet + power/data service plinth;
- cold/dry storage;
- quick connector;
- 0.90 m main counter;
- 0.76 m accessible counter zone;
- knee clearance;
- fold-down evening bench;
- wash-down drain;
- repairable floor build-up;
- serviceable light / identity rail.

## Roof logic
The clear compression span remains **9.0 m** inside a **10.0 m** envelope.
The shell concept remains approximately:
- ~120 mm field;
- ~150 mm local springing zones.

These dimensions are still design-development assumptions, not structural certification.

## Maintainability
One 5 m bay can be isolated while adjacent bays continue operating.

Permanent:
- primary structure;
- foundations;
- civic ground.

Replaceable:
- stall;
- solar fin;
- acoustic insert;
- light;
- gutter section;
- MEP component;
- identity panel.

## Tile / shading distinction
The shell bond and the ceramic shading layer are now separate systems.
The ceramic fin is bolted to an independent bracket and is not treated as irreversible decorative cladding.
