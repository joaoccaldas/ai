## Oct 4–7: M0 truth
Brief lock, official assets, CRS/site boundary, context, sun/view baseline, repo/CI.

## Oct 8–14: divergence
Five concepts, each with plan sketch, section, structure, climate logic, program allocation and one ugly-but-honest massing model.

## Oct 15–21: elimination
Quantitative/qualitative reviews, fatal-flaw tests, select two finalists.

## Oct 22–Nov 4: architecture proof
Develop two finalists enough to compare circulation, section, structure, passive strategy, public realm and viewpoint.

## Nov 5–11: choose + freeze thesis
Select one. Freeze core geometry interfaces and board story.

## Nov 12–25: resolution
Unified model, detail system, material strategy, simulations, A4 object, first final cameras.

## Nov 26–Dec 5: representation
Cycles finals, plans/sections/axon, diagrams, AI-process evidence, captions.

## Dec 6–10: board v1→v3
A1 composition, distance tests, external red-team, corrections.

## Dec 11–14: freeze
Only defects, contradictions and legibility fixes. Final provenance/anonymity/file audit.

## Dec 15: submit
Target submission at least 24h before official deadline.
