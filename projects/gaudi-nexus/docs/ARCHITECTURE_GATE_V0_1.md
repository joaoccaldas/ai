## C · Cerdà Mutation
**Thesis:** Cerdà's rational city becomes the base grammar. It mutates only when structure, circulation, climate or views demand it.

- 3 levels, ~600 m²/floor.
- Narrow 7–10 m bands.
- One band carries hard servicing.
- Top level holds interpretation/viewpoint and must taper/set back.
- Strongest Gaudí-method translation today because form can emerge from force/light rather than surface styling.

**Fatal risk:** 12–14 m apparent height may compete with the basilica and become diagrammatic/formalist.

**Required next proof:** exact 3D height/view test + daylight + structural bay study.

## E · Market Organism
**Thesis:** make the market's metabolism architectural. One maintainable service spine feeds adaptable public cells.

- 2 levels, ~900 m²/floor.
- 9–12 m modular bays.
- Strongest logistics concept.
- Concentrated drainage, exhaust, cold storage, waste and maintenance.
- Cells must open directly to plaza, otherwise this degenerates into a corridor building.

**Fatal risk:** inwardness and loss of civic generosity.

**Required next proof:** food-market service section + permeability ratio + public-edge opening test.

## A · Living Threshold
**Thesis:** two inhabited edges mediate between neighborhood life and monument tourism while preserving an unprogrammed civic middle.

- 2 levels, ~900 m²/floor.
- Low 8–12 m spans.
- Lowest overall section of the three.
- Strongest social thesis.
- Deep arcades can provide shade and everyday public use.

**Fatal risk:** may become two separate buildings; split servicing is weaker than E.

**Required next proof:** pedestrian-behavior / mixing test + service consolidation study.

## Parked concepts
### B · Inverted Plaza
Extraordinary public-ground preservation, but too much current uncertainty around excavation, daylight, waterproofing, fire egress, archaeology and cost.

### D · Urban Forest
Strong shade/porosity idea, but nine-part fragmentation is currently too weak for market operations, acoustic control, weather protection and legible wayfinding.

## Next gate

1. height + basilica visual primacy;
2. section and egress;
3. market servicing;
4. accessibility;
5. structural bays;
6. passive daylight/ventilation;
