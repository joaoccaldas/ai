# Primary 5 m Bay V0.2

## Why this bay matters
The bay is the project's physical DNA. If one bay is credible, repeatable and beautiful, the whole building can become credible without gratuitous complexity.

## Geometry
- longitudinal rhythm: **5.0 m**
- overall bar width: **10.0 m**
- clear compression span: **9.0 m**
- eave datum: **4.8 m**
- catenary-informed rise: **~1.35 m**
- conceptual shell thickness: **~120 mm field / ~150 mm local edge zones**

The 9 m clear span is deliberate. A built thin-tile precedent by the Block Research Group used a 9 m unreinforced masonry arch and documented compression-only form finding and structural analysis. Our shell still requires its own engineering; the precedent only establishes plausibility.

## Selected roof system
**Hybrid thin-tile/compression shell + robust mineral/precast edge frame.**

Why:
- preserves a true Gaudí-method connection through funicular/compression logic;
- makes the support/chassis repetitive and buildable;
- separates replaceable façade/shading components from primary structure;
- provides a clean fallback path if detailed masonry analysis fails.

### Fallback
Keep the same geometric interface but replace the shell with:
1. ribbed precast shell; or
2. timber/steel catenary ribs + light roof.

The architecture must survive a material-system change.

## Integrated systems
### Rain
- continuous eave gutter;
- downpipes integrated with edge frame;
- floor wash-down channel behind market modules;
- future rainwater reuse connection reserved.

### Solar
- replaceable ceramic/terracotta fins on sun-exposed edge;
- fin density/orientation to come from actual annual solar analysis;
- no ornamental ceramic pattern without performance purpose.

### Lighting
- serviceable linear tracks below the roof;
- separate ambient / stall / civic modes.

### Acoustics
- replaceable absorption strips beneath shell;
- exact NRC/product pending acoustic model.

### MEP
- accessible central service rail;
- market utilities distributed from hard-service head;
- no inaccessible decorative service cavities.

### Market interface
- demountable stainless/timber stall kit;
- floor drain;
- power/water/data connection points;
- parts designed to be repaired/replaced independently of structure.
