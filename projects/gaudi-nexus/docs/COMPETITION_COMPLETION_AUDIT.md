# Gaudí completion audit and production scope

21 October 2026. Gaudí is **not finalized**. The market and structural exhibit
have improved, but the exterior, complete interior programme, landscape,
context hero and final board are not yet a convincing whole. Passing software
tests establishes specific contracts; it does not establish a winning design.
No jury score, independent acceptance or winning probability is assigned here.

This is the complete family-level production scope for the current Living
Threshold concept. It is not a final bill of quantities: quantities marked
“design” must follow room layouts and measured clearances. Repeated objects
should be instances of a few resolved families, not individually authored props.

## What the competition actually rewards

The [organizer's brief](https://fundacionantoniogaudi.org/concurso_arq_2026/GxAI_BRIEFING_ES.pdf)
asks for a proximity market and digital culture centre, plus the full plaza
on the Passion-façade side. Its purpose is coexistence between everyday
neighbourhood life and visitors. Gaudí's constructive reasoning matters more
than imitation of his ornament. The [current FAQ](https://archademy.ai/faq/)
confirms these weights:

| Jury criterion | Points | Present critical gap |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Architecture and programme | 20 | Gross allocations exist; usable room fit-out and services remain incomplete. |
| Plaza and urban context | 20 | Earlier landscape was sparse. New gardens/routes are a study, not a resolved entire urban edge. |
| Gaudí principles and logic | 20 | Parabolic model/exhibit exists; roof-to-load argument, connections and climate logic need a clear shared explanation. |
| AI integration and spatial coherence | 20 | Real code/scene iterations exist; the final board must show decisions and rejected outcomes from the same geometry. |
| Sustainability and passive strategies | 10 | Representative sun/force checks exist; actual useful shade, ventilation, water and material strategies need proportionate evidence. |
| Visual communication | 10 | A1 alpha exists; context hero, complete plaza plan, visual hierarchy and final submission files remain open. |

The brief's building range is 1,500–2,000 m². Approximate allocation is 45%
market/neighbourhood, 25% interpretation/viewpoint, 15% meeting/cooking and
15% administration/logistics/services. About twenty stalls, two independent
twenty-person teaching kitchens and eight administration workstations are
programme anchors. Public toilets and support spaces must be accounted for.

The current coordinated slabs measure **1,644.295745 m²**, with gross zone
shares **44.68 / 25.08 / 15.03 / 15.20%**. These are measured slab allocations,
including circulation, not demonstrated net usable area, certified GFA or
approved occupancy. Earlier summary estimates must not replace these figures.

The FAQ explicitly permits a flat site and excludes planning codes, ground
slopes and underground infrastructure from this conceptual competition.
Survey and engineering certification are not invented submission requirements.
We still need coherent access, believable structure and defensible claims.
Animations, simulations, an LLM studio and a walk-through are optional supporting
work, not mandatory competition files.

linear/data interpretation. Verify actual loader behaviour and UVs.
