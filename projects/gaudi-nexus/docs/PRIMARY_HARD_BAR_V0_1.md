# Primary Hard Market Bar V0.1

## Geometry
45.0 m × 10.0 m gross bar.

### Ground
- 11 m × 10 m support/service head;
- ~31 m long market hall;
- 20 stalls;
- current stall module: ~3.0 m frontage × 2.4 m depth;
- 3.2 m central public aisle;
- two remote escape stairs;
- lift;
- accessible WC;
- public WC;
- cold store;
- dry store;
- waste;
- cleaner/utilities;
- staff support.

## Why the market is now concentrated here
Earlier iterations distributed stalls across both bars.

The primary scheme now concentrates **all 20 stalls in the hard bar** so that:
- refrigeration, drainage, cleaning and exhaust are concentrated;
- the civic bar can remain genuinely civic;
- evening community operation does not require reopening the food-market back-of-house;
- trader / waste / delivery movement remains on the service edge.

## Upper level
Working allocation:
- community kitchen / cooking workshop;
- food lab / flexible event space;
- admin;
- staff / meeting;
- plant and exhaust support.

## Current weaknesses
- 10 m overall width leaves little room for poor coordination;
- east exit core competes with the last stall bays;
- service-support rooms need a real equipment schedule;
- refrigeration and kitchen exhaust plant are still schematic.
