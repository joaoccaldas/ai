# Open research questions

## Site
- What exact polygon/limits does the competition provide for intervention?
- Which existing trees/site elements are mandatory to retain?
- What are the authoritative topographic levels and curb heights?
- What are current pedestrian counts and time-of-day patterns around Passion façade side?

## Architecture
- What service/loading strategy can support ~20 food stalls without turning the plaza edge into logistics space?
- How should viewpoint access avoid becoming a tourist queue that displaces daily users?
- Can community rooms operate independently after market hours?

## Physics
- Which summer/winter design days should control shade/daylight studies?
- What wind directions matter for comfort and cross-ventilation?
- Where are dominant road/tourist noise sources and quiet-zone opportunities?

## Human factors
- What evidence exists on dwell behavior in shaded Mediterranean public space?
- Which spatial cues reduce wayfinding conflict between destination tourists and habitual local users?

## Representation
- What do recent winners of comparable single-board competitions show at 3m, 1m and close inspection?
