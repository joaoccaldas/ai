# A+ — Living Threshold V0.5

## Status
PRIMARY SCHEME — not frozen.

## What V0.5 adds
V0.5 converts the primary scheme from bar-scale architecture into a room/core/egress/structure contract.

## Geometry
- hard market/service bar: 45 × 10 m;
- civic/interpretation bar: 45 × 10 m;
- two occupied levels;
- no occupied bridge;
- ~1,736 m² total GFA target;
- ~9 m enclosed-height target.

## Hard market bar ground
- 20 stalls at ~3.0 × 2.4 m concept modules;
- 3.2 m central public aisle;
- 11 × 10 m support/service head;
- cold + dry storage;
- waste;
- cleaner/utilities;
- staff support;
- public WC;
- accessible WC;
- lift;
- two remote stairs.

The market is intentionally concentrated in this bar.

## Civic bar ground
- neighborhood/community ~180 m²;
- interpretation intro ~140 m²;
- foyer/coat/storage ~70 m²;
- support/WCs ~60 m²;
- lift;
- two remote stairs.

## Code-screen consequence
The conservative current-CTE occupancy screen makes Civic L1 the controlling case at ~300 people.

This means the project can no longer draw elegant narrow stairs casually:
- P/160 gives ~1.875 m under the simple non-protected descending-stair proxy;
- 2.00 m is the minimum current drawn intent;
- 2.40 m is reserved until the final protected-stair strategy is chosen.

## Structure
Working bay:
- 10 m clear transverse span;
- 5 m rhythm;
- ~1.4 m catenary-informed roof rise;
- repetitive edge supports;
- column-free market hall.

## Kill conditions
The primary scheme still dies or radically changes if:
1. protected stairs/cores destroy room quality;
2. support rooms cannot work with real food-market equipment;
3. kitchen exhaust/refrigeration plant dominates the roof;
4. structural thrust or lateral stability makes the roof irrational;
5. municipal context reverses current view/shadow conclusions;
6. the first authoritative human-scale Blender/Cycles scene still feels generic.
