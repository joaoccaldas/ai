# C+ — Cerdà Mutation V0.2

## Thesis
Cerdà supplies the rational urban grammar. Gaudí supplies the method of local deformation.

The architecture stays ordered until one of five forces justifies change:
- public flow;
- protected Sagrada views;
- structural load path;
- climate/daylight;
- market servicing.

## Current dimensional intent
- Target GFA: ~1,800 m².
- Ground footprint: ~600 m².
- Ground occupation: ~5.2% of current plaza model.
- Three narrow bands / levels.
- Current maximum roof proxy: ~14 m, subject to reduction after the height test.
- Protected-view proxy overlap: 0 m².
- Current sampled approach-ray obstruction: 0°.

## Structure
- narrow 7–10 m floor plates;
- ordinary short spans first;
- branching / catenary-informed transfer only where a band splits or bridges;
- no decorative structural curves.

## Market metabolism
One western band becomes the hard-service chassis:
delivery → cold/dry storage → wet cores → stalls → waste/exhaust.

This is harvested from Market Organism.

Current proxy:
- service entry → hard-service band: ~36.6 m;
- service-route overlap with the 18 m civic-center zone: ~3.6 m.

### Required next improvement
Push the hard-service distribution entirely inside the service band so civic-center overlap becomes 0 m.

## Accessibility / egress
Concept screen:
- main step-free routes targeted at 1.5 m clear;
- all upper public levels lift-served;
- two remote vertical escape cores retained;
- exact regulatory compliance still pending current CTE DB-SUA / DB-SI verification.

## Passive strategy
- narrow plates support cross-ventilation;
- shading external to conditioned/public rooms;
- roof apertures positioned by light need, not composition;
- current conservative shadow proxy:
  - Jun 21 12:00: ~22.9% of plaza envelope;
  - Dec 21 12:00: ~36.5%.

These are comparison proxies, not final comfort claims.

## Fatal risk
C can become an intellectually neat diagram that feels too tall, too formal and too institutional beside the basilica.
