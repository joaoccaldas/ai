# Primary Scheme V0.1 — The Living Threshold

## One-sentence thesis
A low civic threshold gives the neighborhood its square back by concentrating market metabolism at one edge, civic life at the other, and keeping the middle genuinely public while preserving the Sagrada Família as the dominant spatial reference.

## Architecture
### Hard edge
- market;
- loading;
- cold/dry storage;
- waste;
- wet cores;
- food prep;
- trader support;
- concentrated exhaust/services.

### Light civic edge
- neighborhood room;
- cooking/workshops;
- interpretation;
- administration;
- viewpoint;
- shaded public threshold.

### Civic middle
- unprogrammed ground;
- shade;
- sitting;
- crossing;
- gathering;
- no routine servicing;
- no enclosed bridge assumed at this stage.

## Geometry rule
Begin with two low Cerdà-aligned bars.

Geometry may deform only if one of these forces justifies it:
1. protected Sagrada view;
2. public movement;
3. structural load;
4. daylight/shade;
5. market servicing.

No deformation survives if it exists only to “look Gaudí”.

## Material hierarchy

- ceramic or terracotta solar-control elements;
- durable/reused mineral ground plane;

## Gaudí translation
Not style.

The translation is:
- empirical testing;
- geometry changed by force/light/use;
- material hierarchy;
- structural legibility;
- fabrication technology serving precision;
- colour/material only where it contributes environmental or human value.

## Donor logic
- **A = architecture:** civic topology.
- **C = method:** Cerdà order deforms only when evidence justifies it.
- **E = subsystem:** market/service metabolism.

This hierarchy prevents concept soup.
