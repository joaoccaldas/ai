# Threshold Stall 01

## Competition role
Optional A4 design-prize candidate.

## Thesis
A market stall should not become dead infrastructure after closing time.

The module changes role through the day:

### Market
- 3.0 × 2.4 m module;
- food-safe working chassis;
- 0.90 m primary counter;
- integrated 0.76 m accessible counter zone;
- cold/dry lockable storage;
- water / drainage / power / data service rail.

### Reset
- food surfaces fold/lock inward;
- display surfaces secure stock;
- utilities remain concealed;
- no blank roller-shutter frontage.

### Civic evening mode
- front counter transforms to ~0.45 m bench;
- ceramic/timber panel becomes civic backrest / identity surface;
- low linear light remains active.

## Material logic
1. food-safe stainless chassis;
2. plug-in wet-service rail;
3. plug-in power/data rail;
4. lockable cold/dry storage;
5. replaceable counter/display kit;
6. ceramic/timber public skin.

## Why it belongs to the building
The 5 m structural bay is long-life infrastructure.
The 3 m stall is short-life adaptable equipment.

This separation lets trader mix, technology and public use change without rebuilding the roof or civic ground.

## Final A4 gate
The optional board is approved only when:
- the floor service connection is dimensioned and buildable;
- wash-down, drainage and hygiene sequence are explicit;
- accessible approach, knee clearance and counter use are checked;
- market -> reset -> civic transformation is shown in one reading sequence;
- material choices are tied to hygiene, durability, repair and touch;
- the object derives from the same 5 m bay/service logic as the main project;
- no decorative Gaudí motif is used without functional justification;
- final A4 JPEG/JPG is <=10 MB, anonymous and English.
