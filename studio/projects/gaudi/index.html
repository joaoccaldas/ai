01 / The question

How can a visual concept stay accountable to its brief, site constraints and design evidence?

02 / The approach

Gaudí Nexus pairs a structured competition brief with candidate concepts, an evidence ledger and Blender-based architectural studies. The current work investigates a Living Threshold scheme and a provisional digital world.

  • A structured rubric compares the concept against the design brief.
  • Site context and program requirements are tracked alongside the visual work.
  • Blender and browser studies support iteration before the proposal is considered resolved.

03 / Where it stands

An evolving architectural concept and evaluation process. The design still needs authoritative site reconciliation, structural and environmental proof, and final presentation renders.

04 / What comes next

Resolve the site context and engineering evidence before developing the final competition boards.
