Konam

A bicycle becomes a canvas. A digital museum of speed, engineering and color.

Focus
3D art direction · asset pipelines · interaction design
Stage
Interactive prototype
Tools
Blender · Three.js · glTF · Meshopt · GLSL

01 / The question

How can a precision machine feel like an artwork you can explore, rather than a product photograph?

02 / The approach

Konam combines detailed bicycle models, material systems and themed museum spaces. The Sanctuary edition gives the Speedmax CFR a flowing five-color finish, while carbon, tyres and mechanical parts retain their own materials.

  • Named material slots keep the paint system separate from the geometry.
  • A procedural shader carries the Stained Glass palette across the frame without a flat image texture.
  • For this portfolio, the original 509,624-triangle bike becomes two compressed derivatives: 131,772 triangles on desktop and 44,717 on mobile.

03 / Where it stands

A working bike and material system, presented here as a standalone exhibit. The wider museum remains an evolving prototype.

04 / What comes next

Broaden device testing, document each model’s provenance, and refine the full museum’s navigation.
