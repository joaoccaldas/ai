Studio Museum

A walkable collection of digital objects and speculative websites, arranged as rooms of a museum.

Focus
Spatial navigation · art direction · portfolio architecture
Stage
Interactive portfolio experiment
Tools
Blender · Three.js · Lightmaps · JavaScript
Enter the museum ↗Browse the concept archive ↗

01 / The question

What changes when a portfolio is a place you can walk through?

02 / The approach

The original Studio puts eighteen physical exhibits into a Blender-authored museum. Rooms organize the work by theme; visitors can navigate by keyboard, touch, the map or a guided route to an exhibit.

  • Five wings connect to a central atrium with a featured object.
  • Exhibits connect directly to their interactive websites.
  • The portfolio now gives these experiments context through readable case studies before inviting visitors into the museum.

03 / Where it stands

A working spatial portfolio experiment, preserved alongside this curated project index. The archive includes speculative brand and website studies, rather than verified commercial commissions.

04 / What comes next

Replace more generic exhibits with project-specific artifacts and add stronger labels within the 3D space.
