NOEMA

An observatory for beliefs, rituals and deep time. Every claim has a source; every unknown stays visible.

Focus
Research infrastructure · data provenance · interface design
Stage
Research prototype
Tools
Python · Pydantic · FastAPI · JavaScript
Open the observatory ↗Read the research contract ↗

01 / The question

How can a research interface reveal connections without turning resemblance into an unsupported conclusion?

02 / The approach

NOEMA separates observations, claims, interpretations and hypotheses. Discovery and ingestion feed review gates; provenance and competing explanations remain part of the public research view.

  • Typed records distinguish evidence from interpretation.
  • Source resolution and review queues sit between ingestion and approved evidence.
  • The observatory offers a browser interface to geography, chronology and research context.

03 / Where it stands

A research system with an implemented observatory and ingestion architecture. It is a tool for investigation, with scientific conclusions subject to review.

04 / What comes next

Continue source audits and human review, and make the limits of every public projection easier to inspect.
