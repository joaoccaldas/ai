SŌKAI

The anatomy of a katana, made tangible through light, movement and a carefully built digital object.

Focus
Object modeling · lighting · browser interaction
Stage
Interactive concept
Tools
Blender · Cycles · Three.js · glTF
Explore the katana ↗See the making-of ↗

01 / The question

Can an object’s construction become the experience itself?

02 / The approach

A Blender-authored katana is rendered live in Three.js. Visitors can orbit the object, inspect parts, reveal the blade and explore its assembly. A dedicated making-of route follows the construction process.

  • The object can be drawn and taken apart through interactive controls.
  • Part inspection brings the visitor closer to the model’s construction.
  • The making-of experience connects the final exhibit to its modeling process.

03 / Where it stands

A functioning interactive object study and making-of experience. SŌKAI is an independent concept, rather than a commissioned product launch.

04 / What comes next

Refine keyboard interaction and measure the asset budget across a wider range of phones.
