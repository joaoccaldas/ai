The joke stops at safety.

These are fictional concepts, not operating events. The absurdity belongs in the brand voice, not in real risk management.

A legitimate expedition would still require local permissions, medical planning, rescue capability, environmental review, weather criteria, qualified guides and actual reconnaissance.

The strange part is optional. Competence is not.
