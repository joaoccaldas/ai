THE COURSE · GROUNDED IN REAL GEOGRAPHY

Four stages across Addu.

Most Maldivian islands cannot support a meaningful bike leg. Addu can. Its western inhabited islands are linked by a paved road from Gan to Hithadhoo, while the surrounding lagoon and islands create a natural theatre for ocean racing.

≈ 30 km road loop

So, how does the bike actually work?

The Gan to Hithadhoo Link Road is roughly 14 to 16 km each way depending on the measured endpoints. Addu tourism planning explicitly identifies it as a flat route suitable for cycling and triathlon, with an approximately 30 km out-and-back.

The final 60 km bike leg is therefore two controlled laps, not laps around a resort island. Race bikes move by support truck and boat, transitions sit close to Gan, and any public-road racing would require local permits, traffic management, police coordination and medical coverage.

YOUR HOME BETWEEN STAGES

Race from the water.
Recover above it.

The founding format pairs a private premium liveaboard or yacht as the race clubhouse with a three-night recovery stay in an Addu overwater or pool villa. The exact vessel and resort are partner-dependent.

01

The race clubhouse

A chartered vessel positioned within Addu becomes breakfast room, briefing theatre, recovery lounge and moving support base. Bikes remain professionally handled on land for road stages.

4 race-stage nights · full-board · crew · tender support
02

The recovery island

After the final finish, transfer by speedboat to an Addu resort with overwater or pool-villa inventory, spa, lagoon access and enough privacy to disappear for three days.

3 recovery nights · spa programme · flexible dining
03

The invisible logistics

Bike boxes, mechanics, race nutrition, timing, laundry, luggage, transfers and recovery appointments are handled as one itinerary. Guests should spend their attention on racing and being there.

airport to airport concierge

Feasibility benchmark: existing Deep South liveaboards already operate Gan-linked itineraries, while Addu resorts provide speedboat-accessible villa and spa inventory. No commercial partnership is implied by this concept website.
