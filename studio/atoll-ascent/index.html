THE IDEA

The race is the reason. The week is the product.

Atoll Ascent is a deliberately small luxury endurance expedition: hard enough to earn the memory, supported enough to remove the friction.

Concept only. Routes, dates, partners, accommodation inventory and final prices remain subject to local survey, permits and supplier contracts.

04 STAGES

The equator becomes the course.

01

Equator Prologue

750 m swim · 20 km bike · 5 km run

Fast, hot, immediate. Meet the atoll at race pace.
02

Island SwimRun

~3 km swim · ~12 km run

Water, causeways and inhabited-island texture.
03

Causeway Chase

40 km bike · 10 km run

Controlled repeats, then a sunset pursuit.
04

Atoll Enduro

1.9 km swim · 60 km bike · 15 km run

The queen stage. Finish with nothing saved.

Course distances are illustrative until surveyed and approved locally. Addu is unusually suitable because its western islands are connected by road.
