What counts as evidence?

A planned experiment is not a result. Instrumented results require a commit, scene hash, reproducible protocol, device, date and source. Independent review additionally requires a review receipt. Environmental mismatches never produce percentage improvement claims.

Image metrics, physical correctness, frame times, human preference and financial impact remain separate dimensions. No composite “realism percentage” is fabricated.
