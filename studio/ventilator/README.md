# Ventilator B2B concept

A responsive one-page concept for Ventilator AB, designed as a more technical and commercially focused presentation of the company's current air-treatment business.

## Strategic direction

The page follows a B2B decision path:

1. Capability and positioning
2. Technical service scope
3. Function and lifecycle responsibility
4. Reference evidence
5. Company history, values and sustainability
6. Direct project contact

The concept deliberately emphasizes Ventilator's current focus on modern, energy-efficient air-treatment installation, Service/ROT and long-term customer relationships. Older cleanroom and laboratory material was not presented as an active core offer because Ventilator publicly announced a strategic focus on its air-treatment core business in 2023.

## Source material

Content, facts, contact details, logo and source photography are based on:

- ventilator.se
- Ventilator AB's public LinkedIn company page
- Public posts by CEO Robert Widbäck

External brand images are referenced from Ventilator's own website. All trademarks and source assets remain the property of their respective owners.

## Responsive behavior

- Desktop: two-column hero, technical system visualization, five-stage lifecycle and editorial project grid
- Tablet: stacked feature sections, two-column proof layouts and mobile navigation
- Mobile: content-first hero, touch-friendly service accordions, single-column lifecycle and project cards
- Reduced-motion support disables ambient airflow and transition effects
