A budget is a promise

Set once before the year, and frozen. It is the yardstick every month is measured against. Margin Bridge holds it as its own version — it is never overwritten, so the variance story is always honest.

// living

A forecast is the truth so far

Actuals for the months that have closed, plus a driven view of the months still open. Move the cut-off and the split moves with it: what is booked is locked, what is ahead responds to your assumptions.

The method

Seven steps from history to a defensible number

Nothing in the forecast is typed by a human. Base values come from history; you supply adjustments, and the model does the arithmetic — down to every SKU, in local currency, consolidated in EUR.

01 — History

Start from two years of actuals

Monthly volume, price, discount, rebate and cost for every market and SKU. Seasonality and trend come for free, because the forecast anchors to the same month last year.

02 — Cut-off

Say what is closed

One cursor marks the last actual month. Everything to its left is locked from the ledger; everything to its right is open to plan. Slide it forward and you watch the cost of deciding late.

03 — Assumptions

Adjust where you have a view

Growth, price, discount, rebate, cost, premium mix and FX — set once for the whole portfolio, then refine at Market × BU or SKU. The most specific number always wins, and every override is written down.

04 — Forecast

History on autopilot, plus your deltas

With every dial at zero the forecast is simply last year's shape carried forward. Each adjustment bends it — and phases in over three months, because price and cost decisions never land in full on day one.

05 — Bridge

Isolate volume, mix and pricing

The price-volume-mix bridge walks the margin from budget to forecast and separates the three commercial levers from cost, lifecycle and FX. It reconciles to the cent — a header seal proves it on every change.

06 — P&L to EBIT

All the way down the statement

Net sales, product margin, then advertising, selling, logistics, overhead and D&A to EBIT — by full year, quarter or month, with variance against budget and prior year, side by side.

07 — Stress it

Scenarios, price sensitivity and the range of outcomes

Worst / likely / best are derived from your own history — never typed — combined with correlation, not naive stacking. Sweep price to find where margin peaks, and hold saved scenarios side by side before you commit.

Why you can trust the number

Reconciled, reproducible, and honest about its gaps

✓ Bridge reconciled

It always adds up

The bridge buckets sum to the delta with no residual — for product margin and net sales, against budget and prior year. A seal recomputes it on every keystroke and turns red if it ever breaks.

Every number is traceable

The assumption register travels with the forecast: every adjustment, its level and scope, plus the cut-off, trend carry and confidence settings. A number without its assumptions is not reproducible.

Synthetic, and self-aware

Every figure here is generated from a seeded dataset — no real company data. Swap one function for your own extract and nothing downstream changes. The roadmap lists what is modelled versus production-grade, in the open.
