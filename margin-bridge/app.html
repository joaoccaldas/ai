Margin Bridge — driver-based forecasting and PVM decomposition for a four-market, five-business-unit appliance portfolio. Figures are generated from a seeded synthetic dataset; replace generateAll() in src/data.js with your own extract and nothing downstream changes. Volume is measured at base-period mix and unit margin, mix is the remainder within volume, price, discount and COGS sit on forecast volumes, and FX is the forecast retranslated. The buckets sum to the delta with no residual, and the seal in the header proves it on every recalculation.
