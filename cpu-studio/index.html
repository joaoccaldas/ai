WORKSHOP MANUAL

Start in 1975. Tap parts to install them. Test your build with POWER. Every completed mission pays credits, earns XP and unlocks the next historical era.

Need a missing part? Visit SHOP. Explore completed machines in MUSEUM. CLUB lets you share read-only computer blueprints and barter with fictional museum collectors.

Accuracy: These are specific, historically plausible teaching configurations. The game is not an electrical engineering simulator. Early boards and wired infrastructure are simplified; later levels add power, cooling and compatibility rules.

Privacy: This preview uses device-only local storage. No public chat, online accounts, real money, paid loot boxes or actual player-to-player trades.
